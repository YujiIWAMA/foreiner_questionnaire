1. Title of the study

“An exploratory study of risk literacy among foreign residents in Japan: Associations of information literacy, social networks, and social isolation in a non-disaster period”

This study has been reviewed by the Ethics Committee and approved by the President of the National Defense Medical College.

2. Researchers

Principal investigator: Kimiko Hayano, Professor, Department of Nursing, National Defense Medical College

Co-investigators: Yuji Iwama, Lecturer; Madoka Muro, Assistant Professor (Department of Nursing, National Defense Medical College)

3. Purpose of the study

Japan often experiences disasters such as earthquakes and typhoons. Because of language barriers and fewer connections with the local community, foreign residents may find it hard to get the information they need and to evacuate during a disaster.

In this study, during normal times (when there is no disaster), we will examine how foreign residents’ ability to handle information (information and health literacy), their connections with other people, and their feelings of isolation relate to how easily they can get disaster information and how well they are prepared for disasters.

4. What you will be asked to do

You will answer an online questionnaire once. The questions are about you (age, region of origin, Japanese ability, etc.), your connections with other people, how you get information, your experience of and preparation for disasters, your confidence in evacuating, how you use the Internet and health information, and your recent contact with other people. It takes about　15–20 minutes.

Study period: from the date of approval until March 31, 2029 (the survey is open until March 31, 2028).

5. Why we are asking you

We are asking people of foreign nationality aged 18 or older who live in Tokorozawa City or attend a Japanese class in Tokorozawa City, and who can answer by themselves in English or Easy Japanese.

6. Burdens, risks, and benefits

There is no physical burden or risk. The burden is the time needed to answer.

Some questions ask about past disasters, which may remind you of difficult experiences. We will not ask about details of damage. You may skip any question or stop at any time.

If you need support with your feelings or daily life, you can contact:

  • Saitama Foreign Residents Comprehensive Consultation Center (many languages, including English): 048-833-3296 (Mon–Fri 9:00–16:00)
  • TELL Lifeline (counseling in English): 0800-300-8355

There is no direct benefit to you, but your answers will help improve disaster support for foreign residents.

7. Consent and withdrawal

Taking part is your free choice. If you do not take part or stop partway, you will not be disadvantaged in any way. Your Japanese class teachers and organizations will not be told who answered.

You can withdraw until April 30, 2028. The reception number shown on this page is the only way to find your answers. To withdraw, download the withdrawal form, write your reception number on it, and send it to the contact below by post or email. We will find and delete your answers using the number.

Download the withdrawal form (PDF)

The data for analysis will be finalized one month after the survey closes, so after April 30, 2028, answers cannot be withdrawn. If you lose your reception number, we cannot find your answers, so we cannot withdraw them. After the results have been presented at a conference or published, your answers cannot be removed from those results.

If you send the form by email, your email address will be used only for the withdrawal procedure, will not be linked to your answers, and will be deleted promptly after the procedure.

8. Publication of the study

The results will be presented at academic conferences and in journals. As this is not an intervention study, it will not be registered as a clinical trial.

9. Viewing the research protocol

If you wish to see the research protocol or documents describing the methods, we can show them as long as this does not affect other participants’ personal information or the conduct of the study. Please contact us.

10. Protection of personal information

This survey does not ask for any information that could identify you, such as your name, address, or email address. Internet connection information (such as your IP address) is not recorded. We will not ask about your nationality or residence status. Your answers are linked only to the reception number, so you cannot be identified.

Answers are collected through Google Forms and temporarily stored on Google’s servers. Researchers regularly download them to a password-protected computer at the College and then delete them from the servers. When we report results, any group with fewer than 5 people will be combined with other groups.

11. Storage and disposal of data

Place: Research office, Department of Nursing, National Defense Medical College

Period: until 5 years after the end of the study is reported, or 3 years after the final publication of results is reported, whichever is later

Disposal: electronic data will be completely erased. The data will not be used for any other study.

12. Funding and conflicts of interest

This study is funded by departmental research funds of the Department of Nursing, National Defense Medical College. The researchers have no conflicts of interest to declare.

13. Results

Because this is an anonymous survey, we cannot return individual results. Answers will be analyzed statistically and presented at conferences and in journals. No individual will be identified.

14. Costs and payment

There is no cost to you other than your smartphone data charges. There is no payment for taking part.

3　研究けんきゅうの目的もくてき

日本にほんでは、地震じしんや台風たいふうなどの災害さいがいがよくあります。外国人がいこくじんは、ことばが難むずかしかったり、地域ちいきの人ひととのつながりが少すくなかったりするので、災害さいがいのとき必要ひつような情報じょうほうが分わからなかったり、逃にげるのが難むずかしかったりすることがあります。

この研究けんきゅうでは、災害さいがいがない普段ふだんのときに、①情報じょうほうを使つかう力ちから、②人ひととのつながり、③さびしさ（孤独感こどくかん）と、災害さいがいの情報じょうほうの分わかりやすさ・災害さいがいへの備そなえの関係かんけいを調しらべます。

4　何なにをするか

インターネットのアンケートに1回かい答こたえてください。質問しつもんは、あなたのこと（年とし、どこから来きたか、日本語にほんごの力ちからなど）、人ひととのつながり、情報じょうほうの知しり方かた、災害さいがいの経験けいけんと備そなえ、逃にげる自信じしん、インターネットと健康けんこうの情報じょうほう、最近さいきんの人ひととのかかわりについてです。15〜20分ぷんぐらいかかります。

期間きかん：許可きょかの日ひから2029年ねん3月がつ31日にちまで（アンケートは2028年ねん3月がつ31日にちまで）

5　あなたにお願ねがいする理由りゆう

所沢市ところざわしに住すんでいる人ひと、または所沢市ところざわしの日本語にほんご教室きょうしつに来きている人ひとで、18歳さい以上いじょうの外国籍がいこくせきの人ひとにお願ねがいしています。英語えいごかやさしい日本語にほんごで、自分じぶんで答こたえられる人ひとです。

6　大変たいへんなこと・よいこと

体からだの危険きけんはありません。答こたえる時間じかんがかかります。
